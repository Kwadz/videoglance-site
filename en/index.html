In your Finder

From preview to playback, in the same window.

Three real recordings, without sound. Play each video at your own pace.

01

Press Space. Your video opens.

Select a video and press Space or Command-Y. Playback starts after Quick Look opens. Space and Escape close the preview; Finder keeps control of file navigation and full screen.

Move the pointer over the video to reveal the progress bar, 15-second skips, volume and playback speeds.

02

A quiet control in the Preview pane.

Finder’s right-hand Preview pane starts with a still image. Move the pointer over it, then click the center to play or pause. The ring shows progress: click or drag within it to change position without interrupting playback.

03

A preview on the right, in list and icon views.

Select a file: the Preview pane displays its contents and information on the right. The preview remains available in both list and icon views. To show it, choose View → Show Preview (Shift-Command-P).

Formats and Macs

Compatibility, with its limits made clear.

Declared formats

  • MKV
  • WebM
  • AVI
  • FLV / F4V
  • WMV
  • OGV / OGM
  • TS / MTS / M2TS
  • RM / RMVB

Tests include MKV H.264 and WebM VP9. A container does not guarantee playback of every codec and track inside it; format coverage is still being extended.

Tested configuration

The package has a technical minimum of macOS 15.0. Only macOS 15.7.9 on an Apple Silicon Mac has been tried. Other versions and Intel Macs have not been validated; macOS 14 is not covered.

Apple continues to provide MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP previews. Double-clicking a file still opens your usual player.

Two limitations to keep in mind

Some colors and transparency effects differ from Apple’s previews. After navigating from MKV to MP4 and back to the same MKV, a cached preview may also resume at its previous position.

Availability

Useful questions

Before your first preview.

Does VideoGlance need to stay open?

Once the application is installed, macOS requests previews and thumbnails from its extensions. You can close the VideoGlance information window.

Does VideoGlance replace my video player?

VideoGlance adds Quick Look previews and Finder thumbnails. Double-clicking still opens your default application; there is no video library to manage.

Which playback controls are available?

The Quick Look window provides seeking, 15-second skips, volume and speeds from 0.5× to 2×. The Preview pane keeps its central button and progress ring. Trimming, AirPlay, Picture in Picture and manual track selection are not provided.

How should I describe a preview or thumbnail problem?

Note your macOS version, Mac type, VideoGlance version and build, then the steps that reproduce the problem. The full guide helps distinguish a file that will not play, a missing extension and an old cached thumbnail.
