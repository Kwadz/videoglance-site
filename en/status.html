What has been tried. What still needs checking.

VideoGlance is still a development version. This page separates behavior observed on one Mac from the technical requirements declared by the package.

Which files?

Synthetic tests cover MKV/H.264, WebM/VP9, AVI/MPEG-4, FLV/FLV1, WMV/WMV2, Ogg/VP8, MPEG-2 or H.264 in TS/MTS/M2TS, and RealMedia/RV20. They also include F4V/H.264, HEVC Main 10/FLAC, AV1/Opus, AAC and SRT tracks, a video longer than an hour, and invalid files.

These samples do not validate every codec in a container. OGM/Theora, RMVB/RV30/RV40 and large real-world media still need testing. MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP continue to use Apple’s previews.

Two known differences

  • Video colors, transparency and control effects may differ from Apple’s previews.
  • After MKV → MP4 → the same MKV, a cached view may resume at its previous position or keep its paused state. Returning to the beginning automatically is not guaranteed. In the Quick Look window, move the timeline to 00:00 to start from the beginning.

Trimming, AirPlay, Picture in Picture, DRM and manual track selection are not part of this player. Native features remain available for formats macOS supports.

Download and installation

No public download or Homebrew cask is available yet. The signed DMG has been submitted to Apple; acceptance and final download checks are still required. No download is offered before those checks.

The app will be free, with no account or subscription. Installation, update and removal instructions will accompany the verified release. The usage guide already describes controls and known limits.

Prepare an issue report

Record your macOS version, Mac type, VideoGlance version and build, reproduction steps, and video format. Prefer a small synthetic sample to a personal video. A public support channel will be listed with the first distributed version.
